6. Black Hole Jets Reach Far Beyond Galaxies' Visible Edges

New research shows that powerful jets of energy shooting from black holes can stretch far beyond the visible edges of their galaxies. These jets push into a huge surrounding cloud of gas called the circumgalactic medium (CGM), which is like a reservoir of raw material for making new stars. The jets may decide whether a galaxy keeps forming stars or slowly goes quiet.

Scientists used computer models and telescope data to study how the jets interact with the CGM. They found that the jets can heat or push away gas, preventing it from cooling and collapsing into new stars. This process could explain why some galaxies stop growing while others continue to shine brightly.

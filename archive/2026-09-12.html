6. Little Mercury May Be Shrinking Faster Than Scientists Expected

A new study shows that Mercury, the smallest planet in our solar system, may be shrinking faster than expected. Scientists from the German Aerospace Center reported Thursday that Mercury's shrinkage may be 30% greater than previously believed. They say this represents a loss in diameter of several kilometers over billions of years.

Mercury's surface is covered with wrinkles and cliffs called scarps, which form as the planet cools and contracts. The new findings suggest the cooling is still active and stronger than earlier models predicted. The research helps scientists better understand how small rocky planets change over time.

5. Japan's Hayabusa2 Achieves First-Ever Laser Ranging Experiment with an Asteroid

The Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency (JAXA) announced that its Hayabusa2 spacecraft successfully performed the first-ever laser ranging experiment during a flyby of asteroid Torifune on July 5. The probe fired a laser beam at the asteroid's surface and measured the time it took for the light to bounce back, a technique called laser ranging (using light to measure distance). This allowed scientists to calculate the distance between the spacecraft and the asteroid with extremely high precision.

The experiment is a major step toward future missions that could land on or collect samples from distant asteroids with greater accuracy. Hayabusa2 previously visited asteroid Ryugu and returned samples to Earth in 2020, and it is now on an extended mission to study more small bodies. JAXA said the laser data will help engineers design navigation systems for the next generation of deep-space probes.

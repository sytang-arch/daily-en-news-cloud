6. New Plasma Engine Could Fuel Satellites on Thin Air

Engineers have designed a new plasma engine that could allow satellites to operate in very low Earth orbit (VLEO) using thin air as fuel. VLEO, between 100 and 450 km above Earth, offers better camera images and needs less power for communication. But satellites there face air resistance that shortens their lifespan.

The new engine collects air molecules and turns them into plasma, a hot gas of charged particles. It then uses electric fields to push the plasma out and create thrust. This could keep satellites in orbit longer without carrying heavy fuel. The team says more tests are needed before it can be used in space.

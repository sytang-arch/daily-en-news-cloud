5. BepiColombo spacecraft arrives at Mercury after eight years

The BepiColombo spacecraft is arriving at Mercury after an eight-year journey through the solar system. The joint European-Japanese mission will give scientists their best-ever view of the planet closest to the Sun. It is set to study Mercury's surface, atmosphere, and magnetic field in detail.

Mercury is difficult to reach because of the Sun's strong gravity, so the spacecraft used several flybys of Earth, Venus, and Mercury to adjust its path. The mission carries two orbiters that will separate and circle the planet at different altitudes. Scientists hope the data will help explain how rocky planets form and evolve.

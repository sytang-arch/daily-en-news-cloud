A higher COLA helps recipients keep up with the cost of food, rent, and medical care. However, some economists note that the adjustment can also increase pressure on the program's trust fund, which is already facing long-term financial challenges. Lawmakers continue to debate how to keep Social Security solvent for future generations without cutting benefits sharply.

💡 What this means for you: The COLA is an automatic raise for Social Security payments, tied to inflation. It helps retirees afford basic goods when prices rise, but it also means the government must spend more, which can strain the program's finances over time.

The discovery helps scientists understand how planets are born and how their ingredients change over time.

The planet is a gas giant that orbits very close to its star, so its atmosphere is extremely hot. Researchers used a technique called spectroscopy (studying light to identify chemicals) to compare the planet's atmosphere with the star's composition. The match was surprisingly close, which supports models of planet formation and could guide future studies of distant worlds.

That is about twice as much as earlier projections. Thwaites is often called the 'Doomsday Glacier' because it holds a huge amount of ice and is already losing mass quickly.

The model, developed by scientists at the University of California, Irvine and NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory, includes detailed interactions between ice, ocean water, and the seafloor. These processes can speed up melting in ways older models missed. The findings highlight the importance of reducing greenhouse gas emissions to slow ice loss and limit future sea-level rise.

5. NASA’s Voyager 1 Closes In on a Light-Day in Interstellar Space for a New Distance Record

NASA’s Voyager 1 spacecraft is about to become the first human-made object to reach one light-day from Earth, a distance of more than 16 billion miles (about 26 billion kilometers). A light-day is the distance light travels in one day, and reaching it means radio signals from the spacecraft will take a full day to arrive on Earth. The milestone is expected on Wednesday, and scientists say it marks a new chapter in humanity’s exploration of interstellar space.

Voyager 1 was launched in 1977 and has been traveling through space for nearly 50 years, sending back valuable data about the far edges of the solar system. Its power supply is slowly running out, and engineers have been turning off some instruments to keep the spacecraft working as long as possible. Even after it stops sending signals, Voyager 1 will continue its journey, carrying a golden record with sounds and images from Earth.

5. FAST finds two mysterious hydrogen clouds with no visible stars

Astronomers using China's FAST radio telescope have discovered two clouds of hydrogen gas near the Whirlpool galaxy that appear to contain almost no stars. Each cloud has about three million times the mass of the sun in hydrogen, but no detectable stars or other bright objects. This is puzzling because such large clouds usually collapse to form new stars.

The discovery challenges current theories about how galaxies form and evolve. Scientists think these clouds might be remnants of interactions between galaxies or perhaps dark matter structures. Further observations are planned to understand why these clouds have not produced stars.

6. Scientists Discover First Type I Superconductor That Breaks Time-Reversal Symmetry

Scientists have found a new kind of superconductor that behaves in a surprising way. Superconductors are materials that carry electricity with no resistance when cooled to very low temperatures. This new one is the first type I superconductor known to break time-reversal symmetry, a rare property.

Time-reversal symmetry means that a physical process should look the same if time runs backward. Breaking it suggests that the material has unusual magnetic or quantum behavior. The discovery could help researchers build better quantum computers and new electronic devices.

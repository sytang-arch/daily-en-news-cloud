6. Machine Learning Reveals Warning Signal for Polar Vortex Disruptions

The polar vortex is a swirling mass of cold air that circles the North Pole. Sometimes it is disrupted by sudden stratospheric warming (SSW) events, which send freezing air farther south than usual. These events can cause extreme cold across North America and Europe, affecting millions of people.

A new study used machine learning (computer programs that learn from data) to find early warning signals of these disruptions. The researchers discovered patterns in the atmosphere that appear weeks before an SSW event. This could help forecasters predict cold waves earlier and give communities more time to prepare.

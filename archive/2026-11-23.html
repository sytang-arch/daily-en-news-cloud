This produces extremely bright and short X-ray pulses that can capture fast chemical reactions. The scientists say the next step is to make the laser even more stable and useful for everyday experiments.

The new X-ray laser allows scientists to see the structure of matter at an atomic level, which could speed up research in many fields.
